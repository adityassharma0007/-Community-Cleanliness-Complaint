How to keep our society clean

Small habits from every household make a big difference.

Separate your waste

Keep different bins for wet, dry and recyclable waste so it can be collected and reused properly.

Never litter

Carry a small bag with you and use a dustbin. If you do not see one, take the waste home.

Keep drains clear

Do not throw plastic, oil or food scraps into drains. Blocked drains cause flooding and mosquitoes.

Cut down on plastic

Use a cloth bag and a refillable bottle instead of single-use plastic.

Compost kitchen waste

Vegetable peels and leftovers can become compost for plants instead of filling the bin.

Join a clean-up drive

Organise a weekend clean-up with your neighbours. And report problems early, before they get worse.
